UNiFEST 2027 registrations are open

Teams of up to seven can register until March 15. Each event has its own team limit and fee, shown in the registration form below. Payment is by bank transfer and you upload a screenshot as proof. Registrations without proof are not confirmed.

Eight departments are drawn into two groups. Group matches start December 3 at the Main Ground, with the final on December 10. Captains must confirm squads 48 hours before their first match.
