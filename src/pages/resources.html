Follow the rules in the order your event actually happens.

  1. 4 weeks before

    • Submit the event proposal with a faculty advisor's sign-off.
    • Choose a date that doesn't clash with exams or other large events.

  2. 3 weeks before

    • Book your venue and equipment through the Events Office.
    • Get your budget approved before spending or signing sponsors.

  3. 1 week before

    • Publish your event on UNiFEST with a poster and description.
    • File a security and first-aid plan for events over 100 people.

  4. Event day

    • Register attendees at the entrance and respect venue capacity.
    • Stop amplified sound by 10 PM and keep exits clear.

  5. After the event

    • Submit a short report and photos within 7 days.
    • Return equipment and leave the venue clean.
